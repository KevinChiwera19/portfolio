Making insurance pricing easier to understand.

Nova Insurance is a motor cover experience that lets people compare cover, adjust vehicle details, and see how an estimated premium is formed before sharing personal information.

01 / Nova Insurance

The problem

Insurance pricing can feel opaque when people cannot see what affects the quote. This experience brings coverage options, vehicle adjustments, and the price calculation into one transparent flow.

How it works

  • Compare Basic, Standard, and Executive cover in a clear feature table.
  • Adjust vehicle type, age, and cover level to update the estimate.
  • Review an itemised explanation of the annual and monthly price.
  • Share a quote using a URL that preserves the selected options.

Engineering notes

The app is delivered as a single-page HTML document with its styling and client-side logic. The quote model keeps the pricing inputs visible and makes changes immediately understandable; the interface uses native controls and responsive layouts.

Pricing is presented as an illustrative estimate. Final premiums depend on insurer underwriting and confirmation.
