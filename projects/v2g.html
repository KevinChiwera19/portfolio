02 / Charge & Discharge

The challenge

EV charging estimates depend on more than battery size. Charger limits, charging losses, current charge, and charging taper all affect the result. The tool brings these assumptions into an interactive model.

What the dashboard models

  • Vehicle presets and editable battery, charge level, and electricity rate.
  • Time and cost comparisons across AC, DC fast, and custom-power charging.
  • A state-of-charge chart and charging curve assumptions.
  • Optional V2G discharge estimates with a configurable driving reserve.
  • Editable vehicle efficiency, gas comparison, and charging loss assumptions.

My contribution

Built as part of an Africa University EV capstone research project. My role on the four-person team was business analyst, helping translate the research problem and user needs into a practical dashboard workflow.

The displayed outputs are simplified, editable estimates for research and learning. They are not a vehicle manufacturer specification or a promise of real-world charging performance.
